About QC Small Business

Local roots, local tech

QC Small Business (QCSmallBusiness / QCSB) is a Quad Cities company that serves independent brick-and-mortar small businesses in Iowa and Illinois with hands-on IT support— so local shops can stay open and online.

We’re neighbors who speak plain English: Wi‑Fi that works, devices that cooperate, email that doesn’t bounce, and websites you can actually manage. On-site when you need us there; remote when that’s enough.

  • Devices or software involved (if you know)
  • Good times for on-site or a remote session
